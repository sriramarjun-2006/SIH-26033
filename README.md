## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose FarmDirect, an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) with consumers, retailers, hotels, supermarkets and bulk buyers.
- Farmers can upload their products, quantities and prices.
- Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products directly from farmers.
- Bulk buyers can place large orders.
- AI helps farmers predict product demand and decide how much to supply.
- A logistics system connects farmers with suitable delivery partners.
- Transparent pricing helps farmers earn more and consumers pay less.
Innovation and Uniqueness:

Unlike traditional marketplaces, FarmDirect combines direct selling, AI-based demand forecasting and smart logistics in one platform.

Implementation Methodology:
1. Farmers register and create their profiles.
2. Farmers upload available products, quantities and prices.
3. Consumers and bulk buyers browse products.
4. Buyers place orders through the platform.
5. AI predicts demand based on previous sales and market trends.
6. The logistics system identifies suitable delivery routes.
7. Products are delivered directly to buyers.
8. Farmers receive payments through digital payment facilities.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:
- The platform can be developed using existing web technologies.
- Farmers can access the platform through smartphones.
- Digital payments make transactions easier.
- Existing transport services can be used for product delivery.
Potential Challenges:
1. Some farmers may have limited digital knowledge.
2. Transportation costs may be high.
3. Agricultural products are perishable.
4. Demand and supply may fluctuate.
Strategies to Overcome Challenges:
- Provide a simple, multilingual user interface.
- Offer training for farmers.
- Partner with local transport providers.
- Use AI to predict demand and reduce wastage.
- Introduce proper storage and timely delivery facilities.

## Impact and Benefits
Economic Benefits:
- Farmers receive better prices by reducing intermediaries.
- Consumers get products at affordable prices.
- Bulk buyers can purchase directly from farmers.
- Farmers gain access to a wider market.
Social Benefits:
- Supports rural farmers and local communities.
- Encourages digital literacy among farmers.
- Improves transparency in agricultural trading.
Environmental Benefits:
- Optimized routes reduce unnecessary transportation.
- Better demand forecasting reduces food wastage.
- Direct distribution can reduce unnecessary packaging and handling.
Overall Impact:

FarmDirect creates a transparent agricultural marketplace where farmers earn better income, consumers get affordable products and the supply chain becomes more efficient.
